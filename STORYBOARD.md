# 🎥 Paisa Saathi - Onboarding & Walkthrough Video Storyboard
> **Project Goal:** To construct a highly-engaging, clear, 2.5-minute video pitch showcasing Paisa Saathi’s conversational AI capabilities for India’s underserved gig workers.

---

## 🛠️ Recording Setup
1. **Interactive Demo Co-Pilot:** Open the Paisa Saathi preview on a desktop browser. You will see the **Video Recorder Assist (Demo Co-Pilot)** side-panel on the left and the **Smartphone Viewport** in the center.
2. **Audio Mode:**
   - **Autoplay Narration:** Turn on the **Narrator Voice (Audio-Out)** toggle. Choose **ENG** or **हिन्दी** audio narration. Press **Autoplay Demo** to watch the app programmatically log in, fill forms, trigger call agents, and register transactions, while speaking the voiceover script aloud.
   - **Manual Voiceover Recording:** Use the list below or copy the script directly from the sidebar's Teleprompter box.
3. **Screen Resolution:** Record the standard central smartphone canvas at an aspect ratio of 9:16 (for Shorts/Reels) or include the full co-pilot side-by-side for a 16:9 full product walkthrough.

## 🎤 Top Tips for Pitch-Deck Presentation
1. **Highlight the Frictionless Aspect:** Emphasize that low-literate cohorts rely exclusively on speaking. Our calling assistant eliminates text inputs altogether.
2. **Focus on Direct Action:** Show how immediate SMS and voice notifications bridge the digital divide seamlessly.
3. **Show Off-Grid Safety:** Notice the strict "100% Safe Secured" logo which increases the willingness of workers to trust the application with their daily earnings.
