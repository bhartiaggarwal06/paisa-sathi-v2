### 📍 SCENE 8: Hands-free Voice Expense & Savings Ledger Entry

* **Operator action:** Tap the **+ Add Expense** button, or go to the **VOICE / बोलकर** sub-tab. Tap the large emerald Microphone button.
  * Speak clearly: *"Maine ek sau pachas rupaye sabji aur rashan par kharch kiye."*
  * Watch the engine extract **₹150** automatically, classify it into the **Kirana (किराना)** category, log the date/time, and insert it directly into the state list with an animated entry!
  * Also go to the **"Dream Goals / मेरे लक्ष्य"** card. Click **+ Naya Lakshya / Add Goal**. Tap the microphone button on the goal title to speak *"Naya ghar banna"* and target amount *"Panch lakh rupaye"*. See how simple fields autofill from speech!
* **English Voiceover Script:**
  > "Paisa Saathi eliminates manual accounting friction completely! Manoj goes to the ledger tab, clicks voice, and says: 'Maine ek sau pachas rupae subzi aur ration par kharch kiye'. The engine automatically captures 'one hundred and fifty', translates Hindi semantic syntax, logs it under the primary 'Kirana' grocery tag, and inserts it directly into the cloud ledger without ever typing a key!"
* **Hindi Voiceover Script (आवाज़):**
  > "रोज-रोज का पाई-पाई का हिसाब लिखना अब बहुत आसान है! बस हिसाब-किताब बही पर जाएं, बोलकर खर्च जोड़ें पर क्लिक करें और बोलें - 'मैंने एक सौ पचास रुपये सब्जी और राशन पर खर्च किये'। हमारा एआई आपके 'एक सौ पचास' अंक को पहचानकर उसे किराना की श्रेणी में डालकर सुरक्षित बहीखाते में लिख देगा। यही नहीं, अब आप लक्ष्य फार्म में भी बोलकर अपना नया गोल जैसे 'नया घर बनाना' और '५ लाख रुपये' का बजट आवाज से ऑटो-फिल कर सकते हैं!"

## 💡 OPERATOR RECORDING CHECKLIST
1. **Device Simulation:** For the ultimate look, record the screen using simulated mobile frame borders.
2. **Audio Levels:** Adjust your physical headset volume and enable **System Audio Capture** so that the native Google TTS narrator dialogue outputs and simulated phone calls are captured crystal-clear.
3. **No-Error Fallback:** If your microphone permission is blocked by your web browser, click on any of the **"💡 REPAIR/SIMULATE SPEECH CHIPS"** visible at the bottom of the screen. They simulate immediate natural voice transcripts, making the demo run flawlessly with zero risk of ambient noise or speech recognition failure!
